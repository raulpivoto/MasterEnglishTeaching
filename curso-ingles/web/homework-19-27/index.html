High-pitched and low-pitched voices

🎧 6The same sentence in a higher and a lower voice
Pitch is how high or low a voice sounds. We use it to show feelings: a higher pitch can sound excited or surprised ("I can't cope with this!"), a lower pitch can sound calm, serious or tired. Try saying "Hold on!" in both ways.

FormUseExample
Past simplefinished events, one after anotherI arrived, put on my cream and went swimming.
Past continuousthe background, or an action interruptedI was lying on the beach when it started to rain.
Past perfectthe earlier of two past eventsBy the time I got back, they had already left.
As I was saying…returning to a point after an interruptionAs I was saying, the food was amazing.
She was like…informal way to quote speech or attitudeShe was like, "No way!" = She said no way.

Reporting a claim without saying it is true

FormExample
reportedlyOfficials reportedly met the groups.
according to…According to the council, no such plan exists.
is said / alleged to…The scheme is alleged to be under discussion.
It is claimed that…It is claimed that the message came from the city.

🎧 9Listen while you read (about 1 minute)
Rumours spread online this week that the city of Eastvale will introduce a new tax on small apartments. The mayor's office says the claim is a hoax. According to the council, no such plan exists, although housing prices have risen sharply over the last five years. Local economists say that low-paid and unskilled jobs now make up almost a third of new positions, which makes renting very hard for young workers. A tenants' group reportedly asked the council to carry out an exhaustive study of rents. "People are fed up with bureaucratic delays," said its spokesperson, "and they should not have to take decent housing for granted." The council is said to be preparing a public meeting next month, and a guarantor scheme for tenants is alleged to be under discussion, although officials have not confirmed it. Critics argue that the fear of new income tax rules shows how quickly an unverified claim can spread.
