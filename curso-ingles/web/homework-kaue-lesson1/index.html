3Grammarabout 12 min

Talking about me

What to do · about 12 min
  1. Read the boxes slowly and say the examples out loud.
  2. Do exercise A (choose). Check and fix.
  3. Do B (write the correct verb form).
  4. Do C (find the mistake). Then do D and compare with the model answers.
Present simple
habits, facts, where you live
I live in a big city.
She works every day. (he / she / it + s)
I don't like horror movies.
Past simple
finished actions: yesterday, last weekend
I went to a party on Saturday.
We watched a movie.
I didn't go to work.
like / love + -ing
after like, love, enjoy
I like playing video games.
She loves drawing.
✘ I very like pizza → ✔ I like pizza very much / I really like pizza.
My age
✔ I am twenty-two years old.
✘ I have twenty-two years. (In English we use be, not have.)
Favorite + plural
My favorite color is green.
My favorite colors are green and brown.
My favorite animals are cats.
should = advice
You should study more.
She shouldn't be lazy.
should + base verb (no "to")
Between: The mean between 15, 10 and 5 is 10. · What if…? = e se…? "What if I don't pass?" · Give it a shot = try it: "I'll give Interstellar a shot."
