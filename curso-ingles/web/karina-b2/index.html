3Grammar12 min

Accuracy check

TenseUseExample
Present simplehabits, facts, stative verbsI go to the gym every day. · She doesn't like pizza.
Present continuoushappening now, temporary, annoying habits with alwaysI'm working from home today. · He's always barking!
Past simplefinished past eventsShe studied at that school when she was ten.
Past continuousaction in progress in the pastHe was driving to the city when I called.
Present perfectexperience or result up to nowHave you watched it already?
Past perfectearlier than another past eventI had told you that before.
will / be going towill: decisions now, promises, predictions. going to: plans already madeI'll call you. · We're going to visit Rome in June.
too / so
too + adjective = a problem: "too shy".
so + adjective = intensifier: "so beautiful".
listen / hear
listen to = on purpose (a podcast).
hear = it just reaches your ears (thunder).
make / do
make = create from scratch (a collage).
do = perform an activity (homework).
get + adjective
"I got happy" (change of state). But: go crazy.
feel like + -ing
"I feel like eating pizza." · "I didn't feel like crying."
want someone to
"I want you to be happy." · unless = if not.
Other useful ones: manage to + verb (succeed): "Did you manage to do it?" · could (past ability): "I couldn't study." · would be: "Today was better than I thought it would be." · noun + noun: "a lemon balm tea", not "tea of lemon balm".

A chat about plans

Maya and Leo are talking about work and the weekend. Listen once for the general idea, then again for the details.

🎧 7Maya and Leo
AChoose the correct answer.
Show the transcript (after the exercise)

Maya: Hey Leo, did you manage to finish the report?
Leo: Almost! I was going to send it last night, but I felt so tired that I fell asleep on the sofa.
Maya: Don't worry. Unless the client calls today, you'll have the whole morning.
Leo: I hope so. I've been overthinking it all week. Honestly, I don't feel like working anymore.
Maya: Then take a break. I'm going to a pottery workshop on Saturday. Do you want to come?
Leo: Sounds great, but I'm too busy this weekend. My cousin is visiting, and I'll cook dinner for the whole family.
Maya: Wow, that's so kind of you. I'll see you on Monday, then.

Find in the text: manage to · was going to · so tired · unless · overthinking · don't feel like · too busy · I'll

6Reading10 min

The Disappearing Family Meal

🎧 8Listen while you read (about 50 seconds)
In many Brazilian families, Sunday lunch at grandma's house is almost sacred. Yet around the world, studies suggest that the number of families who eat together regularly has been dwindling for decades. Long working hours, long commutes and busy school schedules mean that many people now eat on the go, often relying on convenience food or delivery apps. Researchers argue that this matters more than we think: children who regularly share meals with their families tend to have better diets, stronger language skills and a greater sense of belonging. Ana, a 34-year-old lawyer, admits that her family rarely sits down together on weekdays. "But we cherish Sundays," she says. "That's when we really bond."
