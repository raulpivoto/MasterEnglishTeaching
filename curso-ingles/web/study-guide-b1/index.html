iHow to use this guide

Welcome! You can do this

While your teacher is away, you will complete 4 study blocks. Each block is equal to one class of 60 minutes. Do one block, rest, and then continue another day. Small steps every few days work better than one long session.

StepWhat you doTime
1 TheoryRead the explanation. Say the examples out loud.20 min
2 ReadingRead a short text, then answer the questions.10 min
3 ListeningListen (or read aloud), then answer the questions.10 min
4 PracticeDo 4 or 5 exercises. Do not look at the answers.20 min

Rules of the game
1. Write your own answers in every box. Your work is saved on this device.
2. Check the Answer Key at the very end of the guide only after you finish a block.
3. Correct your mistakes with a different colour or in the box, and try to understand why you were wrong.
4. When you finish, press Copy my answers at the end and send them to your teacher.
No audio? For every listening activity you can read the script aloud (after trying to listen), or paste it into a text-to-speech tool: the "Read aloud" feature of your browser, the speaker button in Google Translate, or the "Speak" function on your phone. Choose an English voice.
✓Final step

Send your work to your teacher

Press the button to copy all your written answers, then paste them in a message to your teacher. Well done for studying on your own!
