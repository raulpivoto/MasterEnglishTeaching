4Grammar

Verb to be: I am, you are

O verbo to be significa ser ou estar.

SubjectVerbShort formExample
IamI'mI'm Ana.
youareyou'reYou're Tom.

Em inglês, a frase sempre precisa de sujeito: “Sou professor” → I'm a teacher.

WATCH OUT · I'm = I am · You're = you are. Na fala, usamos quase sempre a forma curta.

Negatives, questions & short answers

＋ Affirmative

I'm from Brazil.

You're a student.

－ Negative

I'm not from Japan.

You aren't a doctor.
(or You're not)

？ Question

Are you a student?

Are you from Canada?

SHORT ANSWERS
Are you a student?   Yes, I am. No, I'm not.

Dica Brasil · Watch out for these mistakes

Am Ana.✓ I'm Ana.O sujeito é obrigatório.
Yes, I'm.✓ Yes, I am.Na resposta curta, não use a forma contraída.
Where you are from?✓ Where are you from?Na pergunta, o verbo vem antes do sujeito.
CULTURE CORNER · “How are you?” é uma saudação, não uma pergunta sobre saúde. A resposta comum é Fine, thanks. And you?

6Speaking

Role-play: meet a new classmate

Work in pairs. Student A is Carlos. Student B is Emma.

Carlos · Student ABrazil · Brazilian · student
Emma · Student BEngland · English · teacher
  1. Greet: Hello! / Good morning!
  2. Ask the name: What's your name?
  3. Spell it: How do you spell it?
  4. Ask about origin: Are you …? Where are you from?
  5. Say goodbye: Nice to meet you. See you later!

Now change roles. Then use your own information!
